What you drink is your business. Kiroku is a private diary for it: log a drink in seconds, see your days in a calendar, and find your patterns in the statistics.

Kiroku is built for harm reduction. It doesn't reward, encourage, or gamify drinking. It's for adults who want a clear record of their own drinking, whether that's to notice trends, set a daily limit, or cut back.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Log drinks during a live session, or add a past session later
• Record drink types and standard units (grams of pure alcohol)
• Add notes to remember a session's context
• Browse your history in a monthly calendar, where each day's color follows your own daily limit
• See totals, units over time, patterns, and breakdowns in the statistics
• Count your alcohol-free days and streaks
• Log a drink from your Apple Watch

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online
• Your data is yours: view, edit, or delete any session at any time
• Delete your account and all of its data from inside the app
• No ads

FRIENDS (OPTIONAL)
• Add friends and see each other's sessions
• Hide your sessions from friends at any time in Settings
