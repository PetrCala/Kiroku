Kiroku is a private diary for your alcohol consumption. Log what you drink, see it back in a calendar and in statistics, and make informed decisions about your own habits.

Kiroku is built around harm reduction. Most people underestimate how much they drink. The more clearly you see your own patterns, the easier it is to set a daily limit, notice trends, and cut back when you want to. Kiroku doesn't reward, encourage, or gamify drinking. It's free to use and has no ads.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Log drinks during a live session, or add a past session later
• Record drink types and standard units (grams of pure alcohol) per session
• Add notes to remember the context of a session
• Browse your full history in a monthly calendar, where each day's color follows your own daily limit
• See statistics and trends: totals, units over time, patterns, and breakdowns
• Count your alcohol-free days and build alcohol-free streaks

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online
• Your data is yours: view, edit, or delete any session at any time
• Delete your account and all of its data from inside the app

FRIENDS (OPTIONAL)
• Add friends to share progress and support each other
• Hide your sessions from friends at any time in Settings
