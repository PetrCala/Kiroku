Keep track of your alcohol adventures together. Log your drinks and share them with your friends.

Start a session when you head out, add drinks as the rounds come, and look back at the night in your calendar. Your friends see it too, and you see theirs.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Log drinks during a live session, or add a past session later
• Look through your friends' sessions
• Browse your history in a monthly calendar, where each day's color follows your own daily limit
• See totals, units over time, patterns, and breakdowns in the statistics
• Record drink types and standard units (grams of pure alcohol)
• Add notes to remember a session's context
• Count your alcohol-free days and streaks

FRIENDS
• Find your friends and send them a friend request
• Want to keep it to yourself? Hide your sessions in Settings.
• Report or block anyone from their profile

YOUR DATA
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online
• View, edit, or delete any session at any time
• Delete your account and all of its data from inside the app
• No ads
